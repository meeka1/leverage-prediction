## Sector classification

- GICS resolved: **726** of 3,000 (24%)
- Remaining pooled into `Unclassified`: **2,274**

Bloomberg does not resolve GICS for most delisted securities. Those firms are kept rather than dropped — dropping them would reintroduce the survivorship bias the design exists to avoid. The GICS 40/55 screen was applied at source (no Financials or Utilities appear anywhere in the universe), so the unclassified bucket is screened too.

> Industry median leverage over a single large `Unclassified` bucket is not economically meaningful. Phase 2 flags those observations, and the ranking should be re-checked with them excluded as a robustness test.

### Fields not present in this extract

`interest_expense`, `total_liabilities`, `equity_issued`, `equity_repurchased`, `lt_debt_net`

These are parsed automatically if a later pull includes them. Without `equity_issued` / `equity_repurchased` / `lt_debt_net`, the Baker–Wurgler EFWAMB feature cannot be constructed; without `total_liabilities`, Altman Z falls back to `total_assets − common_equity`.
